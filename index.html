Safety, from day one

This is the plan. The pilot will build it.

    What we do not promise

    We cannot block every screenshot (the Android app will, iPhone cannot), so photos carry the viewer's name as a watermark. We cannot promise anyone is 100% safe, so stay careful, especially on first meets. And we cannot promise zero bots. We remove them continuously.

    What are you looking for?

    Friendship

    Meetups, art, games, trips. New people without pressure.

    Dating

    Meet in a group first, then go further. No explicit content, and no photos or video in chat.

    Marriage both 21+

    Both verified, both 21 or older. Includes a guide to court marriage and, later, verified lawyers.

    Questions

    Is this a hookup app?

    No. It is for friendship, dating and marriage. Explicit content or solicitation means a permanent ban. We cannot control what two adults do offline, so our promise covers what happens inside the app.

    Will my Aadhaar be stored?

    Plan: no. Verification would run through DigiLocker or a licensed partner, with your consent, and only the "verified" result is kept. This is planned, not live.

    Under 21?

    Not for now. The pilot is for 21 and over only.

    Where does my waitlist data go?

    Nowhere from this page. The form writes a WhatsApp message that you send yourself. Anything you type in the demo stays on your device.

    Is chat free?

    Plan: yes, text chat inside the app is free after a match. No photos, video or files.

    When do you launch?

    First a small pilot in one city. No date yet.
